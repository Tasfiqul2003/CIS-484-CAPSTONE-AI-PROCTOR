Professor Question:
What is the difference between TCP and UDP?

Expected Answer:
TCP is connection-oriented and reliable. TCP uses acknowledgements and
retransmissions to help ensure delivery. UDP is connectionless and does
not guarantee delivery or ordering. UDP generally has less overhead and
can provide lower latency.

Rubric:
10 total points

2 points - Identifies TCP as connection-oriented
2 points - Explains TCP reliability or acknowledgements
2 points - Identifies UDP as connectionless
2 points - Explains that UDP does not guarantee delivery or ordering
2 points - Mentions the performance or overhead difference

Student Answer:
TCP establishes a connection before sending information and makes sure
the data reaches the destination. UDP does not create a connection and
is usually faster.
